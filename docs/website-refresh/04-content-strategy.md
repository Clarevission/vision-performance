# 04 — Content Strategy

## 1. Corporate positioning

**Positioning line:** *Vision, safety and technology for the modern workplace.*

**Positioning statement (internal):**
Vision Performance Inc. is a Canadian company building an integrated approach to workforce vision. It combines occupational eye-health programs, a planned mobile and clinical delivery network, and purpose-built software for health & safety teams (VPI SafetyOS™) and eye-care practices (VPI Mires™). VPI's own operations run on proprietary technology.

**Why this line:** it names the three things VPI actually owns or is building (vision, safety, technology), in the order a visitor meets them. It avoids "precision", "performance" and "compliance" as unbacked superlatives.

## 3. Messaging hierarchy

1. **What VPI is:** a Canadian company at the intersection of vision, occupational health and technology.
2. **Three pillars:** Occupational Vision & Workplace Eye Health · Clinical & Optical Care (planned) · VPI Technology.
3. **Proof that exists today:** two live software products (SafetyOS, Mires), VPI's own operations technology, and a dual-discipline approach (clinical vision × OH&S).
4. **What is coming:** mobile vision clinics and a clinical and optical network, stated as planned.
5. **How to engage:** differentiated CTAs by intent.

## 6. Tone of voice

- **Calm, specific, plain.** Short sentences. Name the thing instead of praising it.
- **Evidence over adjectives.** Avoid "world-class", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "seamless", "best-in-class", "unparalleled".
- **Employer-literate.** Use the words HSE and operations people use: hazard assessment, shift, turnaround, site access, contractor, documentation.
- **Clinically careful.** Never diagnose, promise outcomes, or imply regulated services exist today.
- **Canadian spelling:** colour, centre, licence (noun), program, organization (the Canadian Oxford "-ize"), behaviour, favour.

| VPI Command Centre (internal platform) | "Command Center" (US spelling), "our SaaS" |

## 8. Regulatory language rules

1. **No compliance conferral.** VPI helps employers *support* their OH&S program and *document* vision-related activities. It never "ensures compliance" or "meets WCB requirements" on an employer's behalf.
2. **Standards are referenced, not claimed.** "Eyewear is specified to the CSA Z94.3 standard where the hazard assessment calls for it; confirm markings on each product."
3. **Clinical services are future tense** until licensed professionals are engaged: "will be delivered by licensed eye-care professionals".
4. **Privacy:** describe practices, reference PIPEDA and Alberta PIPA, and make no "compliant" badges.
5. **No statistics** without a cited primary source on the page.

## 10. Content not to publish

Testimonials, client logos, case studies, deployment counts, prices, savings calculators, environmental claims, certifications, named clinicians, clinic addresses or hours, and "across Canada" coverage. **Each needs owner evidence first.**
