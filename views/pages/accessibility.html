Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across visionperformanceinc.ca.

What we've done

  • Semantic page structure with landmarks, one main heading per page and a logical heading order
  • A skip link, visible keyboard focus and menus that work with a keyboard
  • Text and interface colours checked for sufficient contrast
  • Form fields with visible labels, clear error messages and an error summary
  • Status labels written out in text, never shown by colour alone
  • Reduced motion respected, with no auto-playing media
  • Text that can be resized and pages that reflow on small screens

Known limitations

  • The client portal sign-in pages use an older design and have not yet been fully reviewed against WCAG 2.2 AA.
  • Our product sites (SafetyOS and Mires) are reviewed separately.

Feedback
