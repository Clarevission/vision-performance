Occupational vision

Vision care and workplace safety, specified together

Workplace eyewear is usually specified by one discipline: eye care or health and safety, rarely both. VPI is building programs that consider the visual demands of each role, the hazards on site and the way shifts and crews actually work.

  • Program design around eligibility, authorization and documentation, the parts employers administer
  • Prescription safety eyewear chosen against your hazard assessment
  • Visual ergonomics for screen-intensive and control-room roles
  • Education and clear referral pathways for workers

Mobile eye care

Bringing eye care closer to the worksite

For many workers, an eye exam means time off shift, a long drive from a remote site, or an appointment that never fits the rotation. The result is outdated prescriptions and eyewear that no longer suits the job.

VPI is planning mobile vision clinics that travel to employers, so assessments, eyewear fitting and referral coordination can happen on or near site with less disruption.

Why VPI

What makes our approach different

Two disciplines at one table

Clinical vision care and occupational health and safety are considered together, not handed off between departments.

Built around the employer

Programs are designed around what employers administer: eligibility, authorization, scheduling, replacement and records.

Technology-enabled delivery

We run our own operations on our own software, built by the team behind SafetyOS and Mires.

Privacy by design

Clear about status

Canadian, built to scale
