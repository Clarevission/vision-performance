The visual demands of the work

Changing worksites

Programs have to follow crews from site to site, not sit at one address.

Mobile workforce

Trades, subcontractors and seasonal workers with different employers and coverage.

Dust and flying particles

Cutting, grinding, drilling and demolition call for side protection and good coverage.

Visual demands

Reading drawings, measuring precisely, judging depth at height and operating equipment, often in one shift.

Outdoor conditions

Glare, wind, rain and temperature swings affect both comfort and clarity.

Wear and replacement

Scratched, broken and lost eyewear is routine, so replacement needs to be simple and fast.
