The visual demands of the work

Safety-sensitive roles

Operators, technicians and equipment drivers whose decisions depend on seeing clearly, at distance and up close.

Remote sites and rotations

Camp-based rotations and hours of travel to town make routine eye care easy to postpone.

PPE compatibility

Eyewear has to work with hard hats, face shields, respirators and hearing protection, and still seal and stay put.

Cold, fog and glare

Moving between heated buildings and winter yards fogs lenses, and snow and open country bring strong glare.

Utilities & electrical work

Utility and electrical crews may need non-metal frames where the hazard assessment calls for them.

Control rooms

Long shifts at many screens, often in low light, with visual demands that differ from the field.
