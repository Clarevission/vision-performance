The visual demands of the work

Digital eye strain

Tired, dry or sore eyes, blurred vision and headaches after long screen sessions.

Multi-screen desks

Several displays at different distances and angles, and laptops used without a stand.

Hybrid setups

Home workstations that nobody has checked, often at a kitchen table.

Glare and lighting

Windows behind or in front of screens, and overhead lights reflecting off displays.

Focus after 40

Reading glasses and standard progressives often aren't set for screen distance.

Back-to-back meetings

Video calls leave fewer natural breaks to rest the eyes.
