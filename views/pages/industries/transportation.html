The visual demands of the work

Vision standards

Commercial driver licensing includes vision standards set by provincial regulators, so changes in vision matter.

Night driving and glare

Oncoming headlights, wet roads and low sun are harder with an outdated prescription or scratched lenses.

Fatigue

Long hours and irregular schedules add to visual fatigue and make appointments hard to keep.

In-cab displays

Switching between the road and navigation or telematics screens means constant focus changes.

Dispatch centres

Multi-screen, shift-based work with its own visual-ergonomics needs.

Yards and terminals

Loading, coupling and inspection work that calls for eye protection.
