Who we are and what this policy covers

Vision Performance Inc. ("VPI", "we", "us") is based in Edmonton, Alberta. This policy covers the corporate website at visionperformanceinc.ca. Our product sites and applications, including VPI SafetyOS™ and VPI Mires™, have their own privacy notices.

We handle personal information in line with applicable Canadian privacy law, including Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and, where it applies, the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

When you contact us

The contact form asks for your name and email address, and optionally your organization, role and phone number, along with the topic and your message. We use this only to reply to you and to follow up on your enquiry.

Please don't send health information through this website. VPI does not currently provide clinical services and does not collect health information through this site.

Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is used for security, to prevent abuse (for example, limiting repeated form submissions) and to keep the site running.

In your browser

This site doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores a small amount of information in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device and isn't sent to us:

  • whether you dismissed the announcement banner
  • the safety eyewear styles you shortlist, until you choose to include them in an enquiry

If you sign in to the client portal, a strictly necessary cookie keeps you signed in for up to eight hours.

The site loads photographs from a third-party image service, so your browser connects to that provider's servers, which receive your IP address. Fonts are hosted on our own servers.

How we use and share information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide what you've asked for, operate and secure the site, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell or rent personal information.

We use service providers to run the site: website hosting (Render), a database for enquiry records (Neon) and email delivery (Resend). They process information on our behalf, and some may store or process it outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry records only as long as needed to respond and to maintain reasonable business records, and then delete them. We use safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including encrypted connections, access controls and limits on who can view enquiries.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, subject to legal exceptions. You can also withdraw consent to further contact at any time. To make a request, email our privacy contact below. We may need to verify your identity.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Contact

Privacy contact, Vision Performance Inc., Edmonton, Alberta. Email info@visionperformanceinc.ca with "Privacy request" in the subject line.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change, and revise the date at the top.
