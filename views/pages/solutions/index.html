Connected by design

Solutions that share one record of the program

Vision is one part of a wider health and safety program. That's why our occupational work sits alongside VPI SafetyOS™, where many employers already manage hazard assessments, training and PPE requirements. Clinical follow-up is planned through licensed eye-care professionals.

A note on clinical services

VPI does not currently provide eye examinations or other clinical services. When clinical services begin, they will be delivered by licensed eye-care professionals, and this page will say so.
