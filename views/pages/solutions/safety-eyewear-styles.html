How final eyewear is chosen

  • Your hazard assessment sets the protection each role needs.
  • Each worker's prescription and working distances decide which styles will work for them.
  • Every frame and lens must carry the markings your program requires, for example CSA Z94.3.
  • Fit is checked for comfort and coverage with the other PPE the worker wears.
