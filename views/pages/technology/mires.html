What Mires covers

Built for optometry rather than adapted from general medical software, with one login for the whole team across every location.

Explore Mires (opens the Mires product site)

Scheduling & recall

Booking, confirmations and reminders, with patient self-booking and automated recall.

Clinical exam & EHR

Refraction, contact lens fitting and full exam charting built for optometry.

Billing & claims

AHCIP and private-pay billing, TELUS eClaims and superbills in one place.

Dispensary & inventory

Frame catalogue, contact lens ordering and multi-location inventory with reorder alerts.

Patient portal

Booking, messaging and document access patients can use on their own.

Compliance built in

Tenant isolation, audit logging and consent management from the start, not bolted on.

For practices of every shape

Independent clinics, optical dispensaries and multi-location practices share the same needs: a front desk that moves quickly, clinicians who can chart without friction, and a dispensary that knows what's in stock.

  • One login for the whole team, across every location
  • Clinical, front-desk and dispensary work in one record
  • Designed with the handling of personal health information in mind

Part of a larger picture

Mires is built by the same company that is planning a clinical and optical care network to support referrals from workplace eye-health programs and mobile clinics.
