What it supports

The work behind every program: coordinating people, places and paperwork so employers and workers don't have to.

Scheduling

Appointments and visits planned around shifts and sites.

Logistics

Mobile-service routing and deployment planning.

Client coordination

Employer contacts, programs and eligibility in one place.

Service delivery

Tracking each step of a program from request to completion.

Reporting

Program-level reporting for employers and for our own oversight.

Invoicing

Billing tied to delivered services.

Internal workflows

Approvals, hand-offs and administration.

Workforce coordination

Matching the right people to each visit and program.

Why build our own

Operations and software, designed together

Occupational health, mobile services and clinical care have workflows that general-purpose tools handle poorly: shift-based scheduling, multi-site employers, privacy boundaries between employer and patient, and records that must hold up. Building our own platform lets our processes and our systems evolve together.

The same engineering discipline is behind our commercial products, VPI SafetyOS™ and VPI Mires™.

Not a product

VPI Command Centre is used by VPI staff only. It isn't available for purchase, and there is no public access.
