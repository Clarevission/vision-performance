What SafetyOS covers

One connected record: an incident raises a finding, a finding becomes an action, and the action is verified.

Explore SafetyOS (opens the SafetyOS product site)

Audits & compliance

COR and ISO 45001 audits run from protocol records, scored consistently, with corrective actions tracked to verification.

Incidents & investigations

Incidents, near misses and learning teams, with questions about conditions rather than blame.

Hazards, inspections & permits

Hazard assessments, inspections, work permits and lockout/tagout, captured on a phone and synced when signal returns.

Training & contractors

Training and competency sign-off, toolbox talks, visitor check-in and contractor prequalification.

Emergency preparedness

Alerts that must be acknowledged, escalation contacts, and emergency response plans and drills.

Documents & analytics

Controlled documents with acknowledgment tracking, record exports and trends across sites.

Built for everyone in the safety program

Workers

Report an incident or near miss in a few taps, complete a hazard assessment before the job, and see their own training.

Supervisors

Approve work permits, verify isolation, run inspections and review their branch's incidents and actions.

Safety managers

Run audits, close findings with verification, control documents and show the program is working.

Records that hold up

Designed for accountability

  • Every change is written to an append-only audit trail
  • Each company's data is kept separate, with role-based and branch-level access
  • Passkey sign-in, installable on phone or desktop
  • Audit questions are stored as data, so protocols can be updated without a software release

Where vision fits

Eye protection is part of every PPE program. SafetyOS sits alongside VPI's occupational vision programs, so HSE teams can connect hazard assessments with the eyewear their crews need.
